2. What data we collect

Stegus is a static, client-side Progressive Web App. It does not have a backend, does not create user accounts, and does not use analytics, trackers, or cookies.

Data stored locally on your device only

  • City selection: Your chosen city (Trier, Berlin, Munich, Hamburg) is stored in your browser's localStorage under stegus:city:{cityId}.
  • User profile: Your EU/non-EU status and arrival date are stored in localStorage under stegus:profile:eu and stegus:profile:arrival.
  • Checklist progress: Which blocks you have marked as done is stored in localStorage under stegus:{packId}:{blockId}.

None of this data is transmitted to any server. It never leaves your device.

Geolocation

Stegus never requests your location on page load. If you tap the "Use my location" button, your browser's geolocation API runs on-device only to find the nearest supported city. No coordinates are stored, sent, or shared with any third party.

3. Third parties

Stegus makes no third-party network calls that include your coordinates or any personal data. External links in the app (official German authority websites) open in your browser; those sites' own privacy policies apply once you leave Stegus.

4. Your rights

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. To erase your Stegus data, clear your browser's site data for this app, or uninstall the PWA.
